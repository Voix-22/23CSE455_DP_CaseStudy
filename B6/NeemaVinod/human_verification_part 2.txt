3. Findings

The available source files support the identification of the Strategy pattern through the pricing-policy interface, concrete pricing implementations, selector, and their use by the booking and staff services.

The supplied final test log reports 205 passed tests, with no failures or skipped tests. This is a review of the provided evidence; the test suite was not independently rerun as part of this verification record.

The pricing implementation also has limitations that should remain visible in the report. The weekend policy applies its surcharge to the entire standard rental amount when a rental interval includes a weekend. The seasonal policy checks the pickup month rather than every day of the rental interval. Duplicate private DaysBetween methods remain in the booking and staff services.
